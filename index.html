    VOLLE v0.11
    2026-07-31 — CURRENT PATCH

    Arena Expansion

    • Fixed the ball freezing mid-air in solo matches — offline and host sessions now simulate every frame.
    • Fixed hosting and joining: lobbies stay visible for the full lifetime of a match, late joins connect, and a dead host code fails fast instead of hanging on Join.
    • QUICK PLAY now opens the multiplayer hub directly.
    • New post-match recap: XP counts up with a full source breakdown, coin rewards, battlepass progress and your daily challenge delta, with PLAY AGAIN front and centre.
    • Main menu now shows your daily challenge, battlepass tier and login streak at a glance.
    • New players get a welcome guide plus first-match control hints.
    • Four new arenas: Aquarium, Grand Museum, Neon Casino and Metro Interchange.
    • Neon Clubhouse is the one compact social hub: lounge, pool, photo stage, and friends.
    • New throwables — Iron Shuriken, Sandlot Slugger, Blockball and Dark Eater — each with its own model, not just a repaint.
    • Three new knives (Dark Eater, Scrap Cleaver, Silver Stiletto) and the full Dark Eater set.
    • Rebuilt first-person hand with the weapon held correctly — no more blade clipping through the camera.
    • Shop and inventory pass: per-model knife silhouettes, 3D inspect for the new throwables, rarity-coloured item tiles, a livelier match HUD and a clearer settings menu.

    • Automatic replay highlights for rallies, eliminations and rocket jumps.
    • Transparent case pity: Epic+ guaranteed on the tenth opening.
